    Write it up with Claude

    StepCrumbs recorded the facts: exact labels, commands, screenshots and your notes. Claude turns them into a clear guide, SOP and LinkedIn post, using what you tell it about your experience. Nothing leaves your PC until you attach the pack in Claude yourself.

    1. Export the pack Every step, your notes, and the screenshots with your blurs and redactions applied.

    2. Talk it through in Claude Attach the pack and add what the recording can't show: why you built this, what went wrong, what fixed it, your hardware and versions. Claude may ask a few questions, then ends its reply with a code block.
    3. Paste Claude's reply The whole message is fine. Or open the stepcrumbs-reply.json file if Claude made one.
